Welcome to **BookNook**, a full-stack digital sanctuary crafted for readers and book enthusiasts! BookNook provides a seamless space to discover fresh arrivals, manage personal reading collections, borrow books interactively, and engage with community-driven reviews.

---

### 🌐 Live Website

- **Client App:** [https://booknook-frontend-chi.vercel.app](https://booknook-frontend-chi.vercel.app)
- **Backend API:** [https://booknook-backend-ivory.vercel.app](https://booknook-backend-ivory.vercel.app)

---

### ✨ Key Features

- **Interactive Book Exploration & Modals:** Seamlessly browse through curated categories, filter books by genre, and open interactive detail modals to preview book stories, visual cards, and related literary news without losing your scroll position.
- **Community Reviews & Story Insights:** Dive into reader feedback with real-time star ratings, detailed user reviews, and shared community quotes that give every book a living story.
- **Smart Borrowing & Stock Management:** Easily request and borrow books directly through your profile. Built-in stock verification ensures copies are tracked in real time and prevents double-booking.
- **Personalized Dashboard & Management:** Dedicated user portals allow authenticated readers to effortlessly manage their added books, track active borrows, and update personal reading preferences.
- **Secure Auth & Blazing Performance:** Powered by Better Auth with Next.js App Router for instant page transitions, flexible image optimization, and robust cross-origin backend integration.

---

### 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js 15 (App Router), React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database & Auth:** MongoDB Atlas, Better Auth
- **Deployment:** Vercel (Frontend & Serverless Backend)
